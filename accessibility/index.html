Accessibility

Last updated October 30, 2026

We want everyone to be able to read our menu, check our hours and reach us. Our goal for this website is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This statement covers the website at indiagrillexpress.sites.tab.bot; it is not a certification.

What this site supports

  • A “Skip to content” link as the first thing you reach with the keyboard, and a visible focus ring on every link, button and form field.
  • Every page works with a keyboard alone. The dish details on the menu open in a dialog that you can close with Escape, and focus goes back to where you were.
  • Buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall on phones, and pages fit a 390-pixel-wide screen without sideways scrolling.
  • Text and buttons are checked for colour contrast, the site follows your phone’s dark mode, and animations are turned down when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • The full menu is in the page itself, so it still reads without JavaScript and with screen readers such as VoiceOver. Search results, filters and status changes are announced.
  • Each release of the site is checked with an automated accessibility scanner at phone and desktop sizes before and after it goes live.

What we know is missing

  • Allergen information is estimated and not confirmed for every dish. If you have a food allergy, please call before ordering.
  • There are no dish photos yet, so the menu is text only.
  • The party request on the Support page and the privacy request on the Privacy page open your own email app and send nothing themselves; if you don’t use one, please call or email us instead.
  • Automated checks don’t catch everything, and the site has not yet been reviewed by an outside accessibility specialist.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site is hard to use, we want to know and we’ll fix it. Tell us what page you were on and what happened.
