Gluten-free friendly dishes

136 dishes on our menu are tagged gluten-free friendly in the app (menu as of October 30, 2026). Tap a dish for its estimated nutrition and allergens. Sold-out-today and new dishes show on the live menu.

Allergen tags are estimated from each recipe, not lab-tested, and our kitchen shares equipment. If you have an allergy, call (562) 991-4320 before ordering.

Allergen guide: every dish grouped by estimated allergen

Leave out (estimated)

Gluten-free friendly means the app's diet tag for the standard recipe; it is not a certification. Our kitchen handles nuts, dairy and gluten, so ask staff if you have an allergy.
