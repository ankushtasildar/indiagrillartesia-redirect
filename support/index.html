      Common questions

      No question matches that. Call the restaurant or email us this question and we'll help.

      Something is wrong with my pickup order

      Call the restaurant with your order number (shown in the app under Account → Order history). We can fix items, adjust or refund the order.

      What comes with a combo?

      Every combo comes with rice and one naan.

      How do rewards work?

      Signed-in customers earn 2 points for every $1 spent on app orders, plus 50 bonus points for joining. Redeem points in the Rewards tab: two free samosas at 150, $10 off at 300 and a free 2-item combo at 500 points. Points have no cash value.

      When can I pick up?

      We're open Monday to Saturday, 11 AM to 9 PM, and closed on Sunday. The last pickup order in the app is at 8:30 PM.

      Do you deliver?

      Ordering in the app is for pickup. For delivery, tap Delivery in the app to order from us on Uber Eats.

      Do you have allergen information?

      Yes, as estimates. Each dish page and our Allergen guide list the allergens estimated from each recipe, with free-of lists; dishes with no confirmed estimate are marked as not confirmed. They are not lab results, our kitchen shares equipment (nuts, dairy, wheat, soy and more) and recipes can change, so if you have an allergy please call (562) 991-4320 before ordering.

      Eating a certain way? Diet and allergen answers

      Do you have vegan or gluten-free dishes?

      Yes. 47 dishes on our menu are tagged vegan, all on Vegan dishes, and 136 are tagged gluten-free friendly, all on Gluten-free friendly dishes. These tags are a guide, not lab results, and our kitchen shares equipment (wheat included), so if you have celiac disease or an allergy please call us before ordering.

      Can I hide dishes with an allergen?

      Yes, as a guide. On Build a free-of list (estimated), tick the allergens you avoid (dairy, gluten, tree nuts, peanuts, egg, soy, fish or shellfish) and you get the dishes on our menu whose estimate lists none of them, by menu section. Ready-made lists are on the Allergen guide (estimated). These are estimates, not lab results, and our kitchen shares equipment, so if you have an allergy please call us before ordering.

      Do you have vegetarian dishes?

      Yes. 148 dishes on our menu are tagged vegetarian, and they are all on Vegetarian dishes. Vegetarian dishes can still contain dairy, nuts or other allergens, so check each dish’s estimated allergens and tell us about any allergy when you order.

      How do I delete my account?

      In the app: Account → Delete account, then confirm. Your account, profile and rewards points are deleted right away. You can also email indiagrillartesia@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

      How do I stop marketing emails?

      Tap Unsubscribe at the bottom of any email, or turn off Specials by email in Account in the app.

      Is my card information safe?

      Card payments are processed by Clover, our payment processor. Your card number is turned into a secure token before it leaves your phone. India Grill Express never sees or stores your full card number.

      What do the numbers on the homepage menu buttons mean?

      Each number is how many dishes that part of our menu has, the same count its own page shows. For example, Chicken has 7 dishes, and All popular dishes shows the 10 dishes our menu marks Popular. The counts follow the menu as of its last update.

      Food allergies

      Our kitchen uses nuts, dairy, wheat (gluten), soy and other allergens. If you have an allergy, please call the restaurant before ordering.
