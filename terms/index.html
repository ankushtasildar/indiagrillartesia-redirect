    The terms, in plain words

    A short guide to the terms below, in everyday words. The full terms are what apply.

    • Ordering and pickup: orders in the app are for pickup at the restaurant, at the time you choose. Dishes can sell out; if something you ordered is unavailable, we'll contact you to offer a substitute or a refund for that item.
    • Changes and cancellations: to change or cancel an order, call the restaurant as soon as possible. Once your food has been prepared, we may not be able to cancel.
    • Food allergies: our kitchen uses nuts, dairy, wheat (gluten), soy, eggs and other allergens, and cross-contact can happen. If you have a food allergy or a strict dietary need, please call us before ordering.
    • Rewards: with an account you earn points on eligible app orders and redeem them for rewards shown in the app. Points have no cash value and can't be transferred or sold. The program and its rewards may change.
    • App balance and gift cards: they never expire and have no dormancy, inactivity or service fees. Use them for food and drinks at India Grill Express, in the app or at the counter. Treat a gift card code like cash.

    1. Ordering

    • Orders placed in the app are for pickup at 11811 Artesia Blvd, Artesia, CA 90701, at the time you choose.
    • Menu items, daily dishes and combo options depend on availability and can sell out. If something you ordered is unavailable, we will contact you to offer a substitute or a refund for that item.
    • Prices are shown in the app and include applicable options. Sales tax is added at checkout.
    • We may refuse or cancel an order, for example if an item is unavailable, the price was shown in error, or the order appears fraudulent. If we cancel, you will be refunded.

    2. Payment

    Payments are processed by Clover (Fiserv) by card. When you pay in the app, your card is charged when you place the order. If pay at pickup is offered for your order, you pay at the counter when you collect it. Refunds are returned to the original payment method; how quickly they appear depends on your bank.

    3. Changes, cancellations and refunds

    To change or cancel an order, call the restaurant at (562) 991-4320 as soon as possible. Once food has been prepared we may not be able to cancel. If something is wrong with your order, contact us and we will make it right.

    4. Food allergies and dietary information

    Our kitchen prepares food containing nuts, dairy, wheat (gluten), soy, eggs and other allergens, and cross-contact can happen. Vegetarian and spice markers in the app are a guide only. If you have a food allergy or a strict dietary requirement, please call us before ordering.

    5. Rewards program

    • Customers with an account earn 2 points for each $1 spent on eligible app orders, counted on the food total after rewards and before tax and tip, plus 50 bonus points when they create an account. We may run bonus-point days.
    • Points can be redeemed for rewards shown in the app (currently a free samosa at 150 points, $10 off at 300 points and a free combo at 500 points). Rewards, point values and levels may change.
    • Points have no cash value, cannot be transferred or sold, and are removed if your account is deleted.
    • We may correct points earned in error and may suspend points obtained through misuse.
    • We may change or end the rewards program; if we end it, we will give reasonable notice in the app.

    5a. App balance and gift cards

    • You can add funds to your India Grill Express app balance ($10 to $250 at a time) by card, and buy gift cards for others. Balances and gift cards can be used for food and drinks at India Grill Express, in the app or at the counter.
    • They never expire and there are no dormancy, inactivity or service fees.
    • Cash back: if your balance or gift card is under $10, you can ask for it in cash at the counter (California Civil Code §1749.5). Otherwise balances and gift cards aren't redeemable for cash, except where the law requires.
    • A gift card can be redeemed into one app account once; until then it can also be used at the counter with its code. Treat the code like cash: we can't replace a code someone else has already used.
    • Points are earned when you spend your balance on an order, not when you add funds or buy a gift card. If an order paid with balance is refunded, we may return the amount to your balance.
    • If you delete your account, contact us first if you have a balance and we'll cash it out or move it; we keep a record of every balance transaction so we can honor it.

    5b. Catering and party orders

    • Catering trays and party orders (including private bookings at the restaurant) are requests until we confirm them. We confirm by phone, text or email with the date, pickup or event time, what you're ordering, how many people it serves and the total.
    • Prices for catering trays and party packages are the ones we give you when we confirm. Menu prices shown on this website and in the app are for regular pickup orders.
    • Please give us as much notice as you can, and at least a day for large orders, so we can prepare and staff it. We may not be able to take a request for a busy day or very short notice, and we'll tell you if so.
    • To change the headcount, the menu or the time, call us at (562) 991-4320 as early as you can. Changes close to the event depend on what the kitchen has already prepared.
    • If you need to cancel, call us as early as you can. Any payment, deposit or cancellation terms for your order are the ones we agree with you when we confirm it.
    • Tell us about food allergies and dietary needs for your group when you order. Our kitchen uses nuts, dairy, wheat (gluten), soy, eggs and other common allergens, so we can't promise any dish is allergen-free (see section 4).
    • Hot food is best eaten within 2 hours of pickup. Keep it hot (above 140°F) or refrigerate it, and reheat it well before serving.
