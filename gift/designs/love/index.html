Love gift cards

3 gift card designs to send love, from India Grill Express in Artesia. Every design is the real card your friend sees when they open the gift.

How to send one

  1. Open the IGE app and sign in.
  2. On your balance card, tap Send a gift card.
  3. Pick any Love design, then share the gift link. It opens with your design.

They open it in the IGE app to add it to their balance, or show the code at the counter at 11811 Artesia Blvd. Never expires, no fees. Gift cards are sent from the app; there is no web checkout.
