Parties & catering

Celebrate at India Grill Express in Artesia, or bring our food to your place. Book a reservation, a private party or a catering order in the IGE app: tap the catering card on Home.

Open the IGE appCall about an event: (562) 991-4320

How to order: call us, get delivery on Uber Eats, or order ahead for pickup in our iPhone app. See How to order on the homepage

The house rules

  • Reservations: Monday to Saturday during store hours, up to 40 guests.
  • Sundays are reserved for private parties of 10 or more.
  • Catering is paid 100% in advance once we confirm.
  • Parties of 7 or more include a 15% gratuity.

Invite your guests

  1. Book your party in the IGE app.
  2. Share the invite link from your booking. In Messages it shows your party's name and date.
  3. Guests open it to see the date, time and place, RSVP without the app, and add it to their calendar.
