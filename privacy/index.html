    This policy explains what information the India Grill Express app ("the app") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "IGE" mean India Grill Express, 11811 Artesia Blvd, Artesia, CA 90701.

    The short version: we collect only what we need to take your pickup order, run our rewards program and (if you ask for it) send you offers. We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.

    1. Information we collect

    Information you give us

    • Account details: your name and email address, and your phone number if you choose to add it. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and either your email or a private relay address that Apple forwards to you.
    • Orders: the items you order, special instructions, pickup time, order total and order history.
    • Face ID / Touch ID (optional): if you turn on Face ID sign-in, your iPhone checks your face or fingerprint itself. We never receive or store biometric data. If you signed in with email and a password, the app keeps that password only in your iPhone's Keychain, protected by Face ID or your passcode, so it can sign you back in.
    • Rewards: your points balance and the history of points earned and redeemed.
    • Marketing preference: whether you opted in to receive offers and news by email.
    • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.
    • App balance and gift cards: the funds you add, your balance and every balance transaction. When you send a gift card, the amount, the recipient's name, the email address or phone number you enter for them, and your message, so the card can reach them and be redeemed.
    • Parties and catering: if you request a party or catering, the date and times, party size, occasion, the trays you choose and any notes you add. If you invite guests from the app, the email addresses you enter for them, used only to send that invitation.
    • RSVPs: when you RSVP to a What's On night in the app, your RSVP and how many guests you're bringing. When someone answers a party invitation on this website, the name they type, how many people are coming and, if they choose to add it, their email address.

    Payment information

    Payments are processed by Clover (Fiserv), our point-of-sale and payment processor. When you pay by card, your card details are converted into a one-time token on your device and sent to Clover. IGE never receives or stores your full card number or security code. We keep only what Clover returns to identify the payment, such as the card brand, the last four digits and the payment reference, so we can show your receipt and handle refunds.

    Information collected automatically

    The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Our servers keep standard technical logs (such as the time of a request and error information) to keep the service secure and working. If the app asks for permission to send notifications and you allow it, we store a device token so we can send order updates; you can turn this off in iPhone Settings at any time.

    iPhone features you choose to use

    Camera: used only to scan a member card, promo code or QR code when you tap Scan. The camera image is read on your iPhone to find the code and is never saved or sent to us; only the code itself is used. Calendar: if you tap Add to Calendar, your iPhone's own calendar screen opens with that one event filled in for you to save. The app can't read your calendar and we never see it.

    We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or browsing history.

    2. How we use your information

    • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
    • To take, prepare and hand over your pickup order, process payment and send your receipt.
    • To run the rewards program (awarding and redeeming points).
    • To show your order history and let you reorder.
    • To answer your questions and resolve problems with an order.
    • If you opted in: to email you about specials, events and offers from India Grill Express. We only send our own marketing.
    • To keep records required for tax and accounting, and to prevent fraud or abuse.

    3. How we share information

    We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, under contracts that limit their use to providing that service:

    • Clover / Fiserv: payment processing and sending your order to our kitchen.
    • Supabase: secure hosting of the app's database and sign-in service.
    • Apple: Sign in with Apple, when you choose to use it.
    • Email service provider: delivering our emails, only if you opted in to marketing emails or need a transactional email (such as a password reset).

    We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, staff or business. If you tap the Delivery link, you leave our app and order through Uber Eats (or another delivery service), whose own privacy policy applies.

    4. How long we keep it

    We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, sign-in account and rewards points. We keep a record of past orders for tax and accounting purposes, with your name, email and phone removed from it. If you used the one-time new-customer welcome offer, we also keep a scrambled, one-way code (a salted hash) made from your verified email and Sign in with Apple ID, used only to stop the offer being claimed again with a new account.

    5. Your choices and rights

    • Delete your account in the app: open Account → Delete account and confirm. This takes effect immediately.
    • Or by email: write to indiagrillartesia@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
    • Marketing emails: tap Unsubscribe in any marketing email, or turn off Specials by email in the app's Account screen. You will still receive emails about orders you place.
    • Access and correction: you can view and edit your name and phone number in the app, or ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.
    • California residents: under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion and correction; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes that would give you a right to limit it. To make a request, email or call us using the contact details below. We will verify the request by confirming it comes from the email address on your account.

    Make a privacy request

    Pick what you want and we’ll fill in an email to indiagrillartesia@gmail.com for you. Send it from the email address on your account so we can find it.

    Enter the email address on your account, like name@example.com.

    Tell us what to correct, for example your name or phone number.

    6. Security

    Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls so that customers can only see their own account and orders. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

    7. Children

    The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

    8. Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app or by email.

    • My list: dishes you save on the menu.
